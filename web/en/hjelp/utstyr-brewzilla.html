This is a reference guide for the BrewZilla — the first of several planned equipment-specific guides — not an assumption that you own one yourself. Kvernhaug Brygghus has one internal equipment profile calibrated against the BrewZilla 35L Gen 4.1 (see the Recipe Builder), and this page documents exactly which numbers in that profile come from the product itself, and which are Kvernhaug's own calculation choices. If you use other equipment, the general brewing assumptions further down still apply — they aren't BrewZilla-specific. See also all-in-one brewing system for the general method the BrewZilla is one example of.

Kettle Capacity

The BrewZilla 35L Gen 4.1 has a nominal kettle capacity of 35 litres — this is right there in the product name, and is the only value on this page that is actually a product property and not something Kvernhaug Brygghus itself chose or calculated.

Kvernhaug's Practical Recommendation: Max Pre-Boil Volume

The Kvernhaug Brygghus app warns when the calculated pre-boil volume exceeds roughly 30 litres — a practical safety margin under the kettle's 35-litre capacity, to avoid boil-over during a vigorous boil. This is Kvernhaug's own practical limit, not a number taken from an official manufacturer specification.

Kvernhaug's Calculation Defaults (Equipment Profile)

These two numbers are the default values the Kvernhaug Brygghus app uses in its own BrewZilla equipment profile to calculate water amounts and volume. They are the app's own calculation assumptions, not confirmed against an official manufacturer specification:

PropertyKvernhaug Calculation Default
Boil-off Rate4.0 L/hour
Dead Space (Residual Volume)2.0 L

Actual boil-off varies with boil intensity, lid on/off, power, and ambient conditions — feel free to adjust the number based on your own measured brews on your specific setup. Dead space is likewise not checked against the manufacturer's own documentation.

General Brewing Assumptions (Not BrewZilla-Specific)

These two numbers live in the same equipment profile in the code, but are really general brewing assumptions that apply regardless of which equipment you use — not properties of the BrewZilla:

PropertyKvernhaug Calculation Default
Mash Ratio3.2 L/kg
Grain Absorption1.0 L/kg

Mash ratio is a process choice you can adjust yourself, and grain absorption is a common calculation assumption in homebrewing generally — neither is a fixed BrewZilla specification.

Source for all the numbers above: the Kvernhaug Brygghus app's modules/equipment.py (equipment profile default values) and the brew day calculation's 30 L warning in modules/brewday_calc.py/ui/brewday_panel.py. None of the numbers have been checked against an official BrewZilla product specification.

The All-in-One Principle

The BrewZilla mashes and boils in the same vessel, with electric temperature control and a built-in pump/filter for recirculation — see all-in-one brewing system for how this simplifies the brew day compared to separate vessels.
