What's stored in your browser

Kvernhaug Brygghus Web stores information locally in the browser on the device you're using: saved recipes, the recipe you're currently building, your brew log, your inventory, equipment profiles, and choices like display mode, language and units.

The recipe you're working on right now is saved automatically while you build it, so you don't lose your work if the page reloads, the tab closes, or the browser restarts.

If you check «Remember brewer information on this device», Kvernhaug Brygghus remembers your name and brewery for the next new recipe. This is off by default, only applies in the browser you're using, and can be turned off again at any time.

You can export recipes and your inventory as files on your own machine, as a backup or to move them between devices.

Clearing site data for kvernhaugbrygghus.no in your browser removes saved recipes, the active draft, brew log, inventory, equipment profiles and settings. Export anything you want to keep before doing this.

Privacy

This data is not sent to Kvernhaug Brygghus. We have no user account or central database, and cannot read your recipes, brew log or inventory from our side.

Kvernhaug Brygghus Web does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics of our own, no advertising or marketing tracking, and no third-party tracking. The site itself does not use cookies either — the storage described above uses a different, cookie-free technology in the browser.

Kvernhaug Brygghus is a static website hosted with a regular hosting provider (Domeneshop). As with most websites, this can involve technical server logs with typical traffic information, such as IP address, timestamp and the page requested. This is separate from your brewing data, which is never sent to us.

If you contact Kvernhaug Brygghus by email, we naturally receive your email address and the contents of your message, so that we can read and reply to it.

Your recipes are stored locally in this browser (localStorage).
